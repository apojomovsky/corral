## How to triage

- Read the whole ticket body before deciding anything.
- Propose labels and a priority for the ticket.
- If you cannot decide, ask exactly one question and stop.
- Never close the ticket and never edit its body.

End with a short summary: the proposed labels and priority, or the one
question you need answered.
